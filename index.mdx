Metrolist is a FOSS YouTube Music client built for Android with Jetpack Compose and Material 3. Stream any song or video from YouTube Music, download tracks for offline listening, get live synced lyrics, recognize music around you, and listen together with friends — all without ads or tracking.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Grab the latest APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist/releases/latest/download/Metrolist.apk), [Obtainium](https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist/), or [IzzyOnDroid](https://apt.izzysoft.de/fdroid/index/apk/com.metrolist.music). Install it on any Android device running Android 8.0 or later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start listening">
    Open Metrolist and search for any song, album, or artist from YouTube Music. No account required to browse and play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in for full library sync (optional)">
    Log in with your YouTube Music account to sync your liked songs, playlists, subscribed artists, and listening history across devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize to your taste">
    Tune appearance, audio settings, lyrics providers, and integrations like Last.fm and Discord Rich Presence from the **Settings** menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  YouTube Music must be available in your region for Metrolist to work. If it is not, you will need a VPN or proxy connecting to a supported region.
</Note>

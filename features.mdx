Metrolist covers the core YouTube Music experience and extends it with features the official app does not provide. Below is a complete feature breakdown by category.

<Tip>
  Most features can be configured from the **Settings** screen inside the app. Open the side drawer or tap the profile icon to access **Settings**.
</Tip>

## Playback

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stream any song or video" icon="play">
    Play any song, album, artist mix, or video available on YouTube Music.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Background playback" icon="mobile-screen">
    Music keeps playing when the screen is off or you switch to another app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offline downloads" icon="cloud-arrow-down" href="/guide/offline-downloads">
    Download and cache songs for offline use. Cached songs can be treated as fully offline tracks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Skip silence" icon="forward">
    Automatically skip silent passages within tracks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sleep timer" icon="moon">
    Set a timer to stop playback after a fixed duration. Supports automatic options with fade-out volume handling and a persistent default value.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Queue management" icon="list-ol" href="/guide/queue-management">
    Reorder songs in the queue or within any playlist with drag-and-drop.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Audio

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Audio normalization" icon="sliders">
    Level loudness across tracks so volume stays consistent between songs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tempo and pitch control" icon="gauge">
    Adjust playback speed and pitch independently without affecting each other.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Equalizer" icon="wave-square">
    Fine-tune frequency response with the system or built-in equalizer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Lyrics and discovery

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Live synced lyrics" icon="align-left" href="/guide/lyrics">
    Time-synced, word-by-word highlighted lyrics sourced from BetterLyrics, LrcLib, KuGou, SimpMusic, and YouTube.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI-powered lyrics translation" icon="language" href="/guide/lyrics-translation">
    Translate lyrics into your language using AI. Supports customizable prompts and multiple AI providers including Mistral.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personalized quick picks" icon="sparkles">
    Home screen recommendations based on your listening history, powered by YouTube Music.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Search songs, albums, artists, videos, playlists, and podcasts across YouTube Music.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Music recognition" icon="ear-listen" href="/guide/music-recognition">
    Identify songs playing around you using built-in Shazam-powered recognition. Accessible from a home screen widget or Quick Settings tile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Radio" icon="radio">
    Start a radio from any track, album, or artist. Radio starts seamlessly when initiated from the currently playing song.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Library and account

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Full library management" icon="books" href="/guide/library">
    Browse and manage your liked songs, albums, artists, and playlists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local playlists" icon="list-music" href="/guide/playlists">
    Create and manage playlists stored locally on your device.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import and export playlists" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left" href="/guide/playlists">
    Import playlists from CSV files or export your local playlists. Upload and delete songs via your YouTube Music account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="YouTube Music account sync" icon="rotate">
    Log in with your YouTube Music account to sync liked songs, artists, albums, and playlists across devices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Podcast support" icon="podcast" href="/guide/podcasts">
    Full podcast library support including channel subscriptions for podcasts and artists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Listening stats" icon="chart-bar" href="/guide/stats-and-history">
    Track per-song play counts and listening time. Includes a time transfer feature to move listening time between songs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Social

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Listen Together" icon="users" href="/guide/listen-together">
    Create a real-time listening room and sync playback with friends anywhere. Uses a low-latency protobuf-based protocol with optional auto-approve for song requests.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discord Rich Presence" icon="discord">
    Show your currently playing song in your Discord status via the Kizzy integration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Last.fm scrobbling" icon="lastfm">
    Scrobble your listens to Last.fm automatically as you play.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Interface

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Home screen widget" icon="grid-2">
    A resizable now-playing widget for your Android home screen. A separate music recognizer widget is also available.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Theme modes" icon="palette" href="/config/themes-and-colors">
    Choose from Light, Dark, Black, or Dynamic (Material You) theme modes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Color palettes" icon="swatchbook" href="/config/themes-and-colors">
    Pick from Dynamic color or 19 built-in color palettes to personalize the app's accent color.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Material 3" icon="sparkles">
    Built with Material 3 including Material 3 Expressive components for a modern, cohesive look.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Android Auto" icon="car" href="/config/android-auto">
    Browse and control playback from your car's display with Android Auto support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quick Settings tile" icon="toggle-on">
    Trigger music recognition directly from the Android Quick Settings panel.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Last.fm" icon="lastfm">
    Metrolist integrates with Last.fm to scrobble your listening history. Configure your Last.fm account in **Settings → Integrations → Last.fm**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Discord Rich Presence" icon="discord">
    Show your current track in your Discord status using the built-in Kizzy integration. Configure in **Settings → Integrations → Discord Rich Presence**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI lyrics translation" icon="robot">
    Translate lyrics using an AI provider of your choice. Supports customizable prompts and Mistral AI models. Configure in **Settings → Lyrics**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shazam music recognition" icon="ear-listen">
    The music recognizer uses the Shazam API (via ShazamKit) entirely in Kotlin with no NDK dependency. Accessible from the player, a home screen widget, or a Quick Settings tile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

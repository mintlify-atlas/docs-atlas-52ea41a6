## Player controls

Tap the mini-player bar at the bottom of the screen to open the full player. The player provides the following controls:

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Play / Pause | Start or pause playback |
| Previous | Jump to the previous track, or seek to the beginning of the current track |
| Next | Skip to the next track in the queue |
| Shuffle | Randomize the playback order of the current queue |
| Repeat | Cycle through Off, Repeat All, and Repeat One |
| Seek bar | Drag to jump to any position in the current track |

Swipe left or right on the album art to skip tracks without opening the full player.

## Background playback

Metrolist runs as a foreground service, so music keeps playing when:

- You turn the screen off
- You switch to another app
- You lock the device

Playback continues until you explicitly pause it or the sleep timer fires.

## Lock screen and notification controls

When a song is playing, Metrolist shows a persistent media notification with play/pause, previous, and next controls. On supported devices, the same controls appear on the lock screen through Android's Media Session integration.

<Info>
  The notification also displays the current song title, artist, and album art. You can interact with it without unlocking your device.
</Info>

## Sleep timer

The sleep timer automatically pauses playback after a set time. There are two ways to use it.

### Manual sleep timer

Open the player and tap the sleep timer icon (clock) to start a one-off timer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sleep timer dialog">
    Tap the clock icon in the player controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a duration">
    Set the number of minutes, or select **End of song** to stop after the current track finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally enable stop after current song on timeout">
    Toggle **Stop after current song** so that when the timer fires, Metrolist finishes the current track before pausing instead of cutting off mid-song.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally enable fade-out">
    Toggle **Fade out** to gradually reduce volume over the last 60 seconds before the timer fires (or over the remaining duration of the current song when using **End of song** mode).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Automatic sleep timer

Configure a recurring sleep timer schedule under **Settings → Player & Audio → Sleep timer**.

- **Daily** — activates every day between a start and end time (e.g., 22:00 to 06:00).
- **Custom** — choose specific days of the week, each with its own start and end time window.

<Tip>
  Enable the automatic sleep timer with a 22:00 start time for hands-free bedtime listening. Combine it with **Fade out** so the music fades to silence rather than stopping abruptly.
</Tip>

## Music alarm

Added in v13.4.0, the music alarm lets you schedule Metrolist to start playing music at a specific time — useful as a wake-up alarm or a scheduled listening reminder.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the alarm settings">
    Go to **Settings → Player & Audio → Alarm** to access the music alarm scheduler.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an alarm">
    Tap **Add alarm** and set the time using the time picker. Choose the days of the week the alarm should repeat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a playlist">
    Pick a local playlist to play when the alarm fires. You can also enable **Random** mode to shuffle the playlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    The alarm requires the **Schedule exact alarms** permission on Android 12 and later. Metrolist will prompt you to grant it from the system settings. Disabling battery optimization for Metrolist is also recommended so the alarm fires reliably.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The music alarm is separate from the sleep timer. The sleep timer stops playback after a set duration; the music alarm starts playback at a scheduled time.
</Note>

## Skip silence

When enabled, Metrolist detects and skips over silent segments within a track using ExoPlayer's `SilenceSkippingAudioProcessor`. Enable it under **Settings → Player → Skip silence**.

An additional **Skip silence instantly** sub-option skips silence without a gradual transition. This option is only available when **Skip silence** is already on.

## Audio quality

Go to **Settings → Player → Audio quality** to choose between:

- **Auto** — selects the best format available given your current connection (default)
- **High** — always requests the highest available bitrate
- **Low** — requests a lower bitrate to reduce data usage

<Info>
  The selected quality applies to both streaming and downloads. See [Audio settings](/config/audio-settings) for related options such as audio normalization, crossfade, and the equalizer.
</Info>

## Google Cast / Chromecast

<Warning>
  Google Cast is only available in the GMS (Google Mobile Services) build of Metrolist. It is not included in the FOSS or F-Droid builds.
</Warning>

If you installed the GMS build, you can enable Cast support under **Settings → Player → Google Cast**. Once enabled, a Cast icon appears in the player when a Chromecast or Cast-compatible device is detected on your network. Tap it to route audio to that device.

## Additional player settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Crossfade">
    Smoothly blend the end of one track into the start of the next. Enable it under **Settings → Player → Crossfade** and set a duration between 1 and 15 seconds. A gapless option is also available to remove silence between tracks without audible overlap.

    Note: audio offload is automatically disabled when crossfade is active.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Audio normalization">
    Levels the volume across tracks so loud songs do not sound louder than quieter ones. Enabled by default under **Settings → Player → Audio normalization**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Audio offload">
    Offloads audio decoding to dedicated hardware, which can reduce battery consumption during long listening sessions. Enable it under **Settings → Player → Audio offload**. Not available when crossfade is on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auto-download on like">
    Automatically downloads a song to local storage whenever you like it. Enable under **Settings → Player & Audio → Auto-download on like**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Resume on Bluetooth connect">
    Automatically resumes playback when a Bluetooth audio device connects. Enable under **Settings → Player & Audio → Resume on Bluetooth connect**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pause on mute">
    Pauses playback when the device volume is set to zero. Enable under **Settings → Player & Audio → Pause on mute**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

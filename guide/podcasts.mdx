Metrolist added full podcast library support in v13.2.0. You can save podcast shows, browse episodes, subscribe to channels, and play episodes just like songs — including background playback and downloading for offline listening.

<Note>
  Podcast content is served by YouTube Music. An active connection to YouTube Music is required to load new episodes and show details. Downloaded episodes are available offline.
</Note>

## Finding podcasts

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Use the main search bar and search for a podcast name. Switch to the **Podcasts** result tab if one appears, or tap a podcast show result to open its episode list.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Explore" icon="compass">
    Browse the **Explore** section from the home screen to discover trending and featured podcasts from YouTube Music.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Saving a podcast to your library

When you open a podcast show page, tap the **Save** button (bookmark icon) to add it to your library. Saved podcasts appear in **Library → Podcasts → Episodes** tab under your subscribed shows.

The podcast is stored in the local `podcast` database table. The `bookmarkedAt` field is set when you save it, and cleared when you remove it. Metrolist syncs saves and removals with YouTube Music using the `likePlaylist` API.

## Subscribing to a podcast channel

In addition to saving a podcast show, you can subscribe to the podcast's YouTube Music channel (added in v13.3.0). Subscribed channels appear in **Library → Podcasts → Channels** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a podcast show page">
    Search for or browse to a podcast and tap it to open the show page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe to the channel">
    Tap the **Subscribe** button on the show page. This follows the podcast's host channel on YouTube Music.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View subscribed channels">
    Go to **Library → Podcasts** and tap the **Channels** chip to see all channels you have subscribed to. Tap any channel to open its artist page on YouTube Music.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Podcasts screen

Tap **Podcasts** in the Library filter chips to open the podcast section. It has three sub-filters:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Episodes">
    Shows two automatic playlists at the top — **New Episodes** (your RDPN feed from YouTube Music) and **Episodes for Later** (your saved episodes queue) — followed by a list of podcast shows you have saved to your library. Tap any show to open its full episode list.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Channels">
    Shows the podcast host channels you have subscribed to. Each entry displays the channel avatar and name. Tap a channel to open its YouTube Music artist page. If you have no subscriptions, a "No subscribed channels" message is shown.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Downloaded">
    Shows all podcast episodes you have downloaded to your device. You can sort downloaded episodes by date added, name, artist, or play time. A shuffle FAB appears when you have at least one downloaded episode.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Pull down on any tab to refresh podcast data from YouTube Music.

## Playing podcast episodes

Playing an episode works the same way as playing a song:

- **Tap an episode** in a show's episode list to start playback and queue the remaining episodes.
- **Tap a downloaded episode** in the Downloaded tab to play it from device storage.
- Playback resumes from where you left off — the `playbackPosition` field in the database tracks your position per episode.
- Background playback, sleep timer, and all other player features work with podcast episodes.

## Downloading episodes

Long-press any episode or tap **⋮** and select **Download**. Downloaded episodes appear in the **Downloaded** tab of the Podcasts screen and can be played without an internet connection.

## Pinning a podcast to speed dial

You can pin any saved podcast show to speed dial for quick access from the home screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Podcasts Episodes tab">
    Go to **Library → Podcasts → Episodes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap ⋮ on a podcast show">
    Tap the three-dot menu next to any saved podcast show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Pin to speed dial">
    Select **Pin to speed dial**. The podcast is stored in the `speed_dial_item` table with `type = "PLAYLIST"`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To unpin, tap **⋮** on the same show and select **Unpin from speed dial**.

## Removing a podcast from your library

Tap **⋮** on any saved podcast show in the Episodes tab and select **Remove from library**. This clears the `bookmarkedAt` field locally and calls the YouTube Music API to unsave the podcast. It does not unsubscribe you from the channel.

## Logged-out users

Podcast browsing and playback work even when you are not logged in to a YouTube Music account. This was fixed in v13.3.0. You can search for and play podcasts, save episodes for later, and download episodes without an account.

<Info>
  When not logged in, saves and subscriptions are stored only on your device and are not synced to YouTube Music.
</Info>

## Login is optional

You do not need a YouTube Music account to use Metrolist. The app works without login for browsing, searching, and playing music from YouTube Music.

Signing in unlocks account-linked features:

- **Liked songs** — access and sync your liked tracks
- **Playlists** — view and play your YouTube Music playlists
- **Subscribed artists** — browse artists you follow
- **Albums** — access your saved albums
- **Listening history** — continue where you left off
- **Podcasts** — access subscribed podcast shows and channels

<Warning>
  Login requires a **YouTube Music** account — a standard Google account alone is not sufficient. YouTube Music must be available in your region. If it is not, see [Regional restrictions](/setup/regional-restrictions).
</Warning>

## How to sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation bar or from the side drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Account">
    Tap **Account** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with YouTube Music">
    Tap **Sign in**. A browser-based login screen opens, loading the Google sign-in page for YouTube Music (`accounts.google.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Google sign-in flow">
    Enter your Google account credentials and complete any two-factor authentication steps. Once you land on `music.youtube.com`, Metrolist detects the successful login, stores your session locally, and restarts the app to apply your account state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After a successful login, Metrolist restarts automatically. This is expected — the app reloads to apply your account session throughout the interface.
</Note>

## How to sign out

1. Open **Settings** → **Account**.
2. Tap **Sign out**.
3. A dialog will ask whether you want to **keep** or **clear** your local library data (downloaded songs, local playlists, and cached content). Choose the option that suits you, then confirm.

## Privacy

Your credentials are never sent to Metrolist's servers. The login flow happens entirely inside an in-app WebView connected directly to Google's authentication pages. Your session cookie and account data are stored **locally on your device** only and are not shared with any third party.

## System requirements

Metrolist requires **Android 8.0 (API level 26)** or later. It runs on phones and tablets. No Google account or Google Play Services is required for the default FOSS build.

## Build flavors

Metrolist is distributed in three variants. Choose the one that fits your setup:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="FOSS (default)" icon="shield">
    No Google Play Services required. Google Cast is not available. Includes the built-in updater. Recommended for most users.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GMS" icon="cast">
    Adds Google Cast support for streaming to Chromecast and Cast-enabled devices. Requires Google Play Services to be installed on your device.
  </Card>
  <Card title="IzzyOnDroid" icon="store">
    Same as FOSS but with the built-in updater disabled — IzzyOnDroid handles updates automatically through its repository.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The IzzyOnDroid variant has the built-in updater disabled because the store manages updates on your behalf. If you install from IzzyOnDroid, update through the IzzyOnDroid app or F-Droid client instead.
</Note>

## Install Metrolist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the APK">
    Get the latest release from one of the official sources:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="GitHub Releases" icon="github" href="https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist/releases/latest/download/Metrolist.apk">
        Direct APK download from the official GitHub repository. Always up to date.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Obtainium" icon="rotate" href="https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist/">
        Tracks GitHub Releases and notifies you when a new version is available.
      </Card>
      <Card title="IzzyOnDroid" icon="store" href="https://apt.izzysoft.de/fdroid/index/apk/com.metrolist.music">
        F-Droid-compatible repository. Delivers the IzzyOnDroid variant.
      </Card>
      <Card title="OpenAPK" icon="box" href="https://www.openapk.net/metrolist/com.metrolist.music/">
        Third-party APK mirror.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Allow installation from unknown sources">
    Because Metrolist is not distributed through the Google Play Store, Android requires you to permit APK installs from outside the store.

    1. Open **Settings** on your device.
    2. Go to **Apps** (or **Application management**) → find your browser or file manager.
    3. Tap **Install unknown apps** and enable **Allow from this source**.

    The exact path varies by device manufacturer and Android version. On some devices you will see a prompt automatically when you open the APK.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the downloaded APK and install">
    Locate the downloaded `.apk` file in your notifications or in your **Downloads** folder, tap it, and follow the on-screen prompts to complete installation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If YouTube Music is unavailable in your region, Metrolist will not work without a **VPN or proxy** connecting to a supported region.
</Warning>

## Why regional restrictions apply

Metrolist streams content directly from YouTube Music. It does not host any music itself. Because YouTube Music has a limited geographic rollout, access depends entirely on whether Google has made the service available in your country.

## How to detect the problem

If YouTube Music is not available in your region, Metrolist will show one of the following:

- Search results return nothing, or an error message
- The home feed fails to load
- Playback fails immediately when you try to play a track

These symptoms can also indicate a network issue, so first confirm your internet connection is working, then check whether your region is supported.

## Supported regions

YouTube Music is officially available in the following major regions (among others):

  <Accordion title="Americas">

  <Accordion title="Europe">

  <Accordion title="Asia-Pacific">

<Note>
  This list reflects known availability at the time of writing. Google updates the YouTube Music rollout periodically. Check the [YouTube Music availability page](https://support.google.com/youtubemusic/answer/9430293) for the current authoritative list.
</Note>

## Using a VPN or proxy

If YouTube Music is not available in your region, connect to a VPN server in a supported country before opening Metrolist.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install a VPN app">
    Choose a VPN provider and install its Android app. Both free options (such as Proton VPN's free tier) and paid services work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to a supported region">
    Select a server in a supported country — the United States or United Kingdom are reliable choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Metrolist">
    Launch Metrolist after the VPN connects. Search, browsing, and playback should work normally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The VPN must remain active while you are streaming. If you disconnect from the VPN during playback, the stream will fail. Cached or downloaded tracks continue to play offline without a VPN connection.
</Warning>

## Offline downloads as an alternative

If you have intermittent or restricted access, consider downloading tracks for offline listening while connected through a VPN. Downloaded content plays back without any network connection.
